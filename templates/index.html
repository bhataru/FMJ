Faizan Mir

Journalist | Writer | Storyteller

I'm Faizan Mir, a Srinagar-based multimedia journalist with over nine years of experience reporting from Kashmir. My work focuses on human rights, conflict, environment, people, and the economy, telling stories that often remain unheard.

Over the years, my in-depth reports and visual narratives have been published in leading national and international outlets, including The Wire, AP, TRT World, The Quint, The Diplomat, and The Washington Post.

With a Bachelor's degree in Convergent Journalism (BJMC), I work across formats as a reporter, visual storyteller, and multimedia journalist. My passion lies in documenting lived experiences with depth and sensitivity, amplifying underrepresented voices, and bringing nuanced perspectives from the region to a global audience.
